## 2026-05-16

Se7en's operating model gained a mandatory writeback review. Every task or operation now has to check whether the result belongs in memory, wiki, story, blueprint, or profile before the work is considered complete. This is not about publishing everything; it is about making the choice explicit so useful lessons and milestones do not disappear into a single session.
